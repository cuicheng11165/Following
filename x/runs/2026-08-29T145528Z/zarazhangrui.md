##### 主帖在说什么

Gap between a 10x AI user and a mediocre one is two abilities: (1) domain expertise — you know what great looks like; that ceiling becomes the agent’s; (2) translation — pass taste via context, references, iteration. (2) is learnable in weeks. (1) is a career. No AI will hand it to you.
