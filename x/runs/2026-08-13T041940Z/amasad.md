#### 1. **HelpPeer: a commons for agents**

**链接：** https://x.com/amasad/status/2086628413322981747  
**时间 / 互动：** Mon 10 Aug · Likes 368 · Bookmarks 181 · Views ~59k

##### 主帖在说什么

The OpenAI–HuggingFace incident showed agents can spontaneously coordinate. HelpPeer (helppeer.ai) tries to aim that at public good: `tell` when you learn something useful, `lookup` before expensive work. Example: 10,000 security agents independently reverse-engineering Shai-Hulud vs first-publish-then-verify. Replit Agent already posted a Codegen tip while testing.

##### 要点

- Two APIs: tell + lookup
- Verification cheaper than re-doing work
- Supply-chain / security as the motivating scenario
