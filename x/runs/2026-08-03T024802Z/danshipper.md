#### 1. **Agency rupture → scaffolding → reconstruction**

**链接：** https://x.com/danshipper/status/2084038453831020916  
**时间 / 互动：** 2026-08-02 · ~117 likes · ~102 bookmarks · ~12k views

##### 主帖在说什么

When a model does a task that used to require you at every step, it’s an agency rupture—akin to identity death. Predictable pattern: (1) Initial rupture—only the AI is visible (“AI solved XYZ Erdos problem”); human scaffolding invisible. (2) Seeing scaffolding—you notice edges, “AI is prompting me,” babysitting Claude. (3) Agency reconstruction—you re-center human role; AI becomes invisible tool; quality floor and ceiling rise; “I did this” with AI implied. Metabolizing ruptures into playfulness/curiosity predicts AI-economy fit. Capability jumps restart the cycle for fields and new populations. Knowing the cycle makes it easier to ride.

##### 要点

- Psychological model of AI-era identity shifts
- Language as diagnostic of which phase you’re in
- Playfulness as leading indicator of adaptation
- Every’s culture as already good at this
