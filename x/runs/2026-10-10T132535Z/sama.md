#### 5. **Two ways AI progress goes badly**

**链接：** https://x.com/sama/status/2099352016988614852
**时间 / 互动（如有）：** Mon, 14 Sep 2026 04:18:19 GMT; Likes=21399, Reposts=2179, Replies=4707, Bookmarks=4807, Views=4722452

##### 主帖在说什么

This is the second post in a pair. The first says American frontier labs have to act responsibly as progress steepens, welcomes a federal safety framework without waiting on an antitrust exemption, and says OpenAI now writes explicit safety cases before frontier RL runs that should significantly increase capability. Pacing is not stopping. It should be slower than it otherwise could be, safety cases and monitoring have real costs, and competitive pressure is not a reason to let capabilities get ahead of alignment. Government help, he says, is for international coordination. The follow-up names two bad endings. First, losing control of the future to AI. He says OpenAI is "unapologetically on Team Humanity" and that alignment has to stay ahead of capability. Second, too much concentration of power, if one person or company uses a very powerful model to impress their worldview on everyone else. The narrow path also includes not letting one country or one lab hold too much power.

##### 要点

- Two risks, stated as a pair: loss of control, and concentrated power.
- "Team Humanity" is his phrase for the first risk.
- He applies the second risk to countries and labs, not only to a single CEO.
- The parent post shifts safety work from release-time policies to development-time safety cases.
- He says progress continues, and that it should be slower than the maximum possible pace.
