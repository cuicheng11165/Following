#### 1. **Convergence to the mean**

**链接：** https://x.com/ryolu_/status/2106337039201505453
**时间 / 互动（如有）：** Sat, 03 Oct 2026 10:54:18 GMT; Likes=2670 Replies=130 Reposts=350 Bookmarks=2173 Views=305464

##### 主帖在说什么

Lu's essay argues that when everyone looks at everyone else to decide what to make, they stop making anything of their own. Copying language, aesthetics, and features gets called learning from the market. The mean is not evil; it is where things land once the reasons to differ are removed. Numbers are easier to compare than feelings, so people make things that produce numbers, and eventually the feedback authors the work. AI makes that loop frictionless: access to every reference is not the same as having a perspective, because a model has not lived the life that makes one detail unbearable. Ship, measure, copy, feed it back, and you get "a tidal wave of mid." He wants software treated as a medium the way music and film are. A piece of software expresses beliefs about attention, freedom to change, and whether a task feels like paperwork or play. Artists absorb influences into a point of view; copying to avoid developing one is the failure mode. If almost anything can be made, deciding what deserves to exist matters more, not less. He posted the essay on his site in a follow-up: https://ryo.lu/journal/convergence-to-the-mean

##### 要点

- The mean is the residue after reasons to be different are removed.
- Metrics start informing the work and then start authoring it.
- AI can supply every reference and still have no particular life behind a choice.
- The loop he names is ship, measure, copy, feed back.
- Software can carry a point of view the way other media do.
- Speed increases the number of judgment calls; it does not remove them.

#### 3. **Remembering Steve**

**链接：** https://x.com/ryolu_/status/2107246891977335049
**时间 / 互动（如有）：** Mon, 05 Oct 2026 23:09:44 GMT; Likes=933 Replies=32 Reposts=47 Bookmarks=231 Views=37275

##### 主帖在说什么

A personal essay on Steve Jobs. Lu grew up with beige PCs, saw an iMac G4 after moving to Canada, and could not afford a Mac, so he restyled his PC until that became how he learned interface design. He burned DVDs of keynotes and studied them. His first iPod was a black nano. Since Jobs died, he feels the industry stuck in refinement: better supply chains, more ways to keep people scrolling, talent spent on tiny improvements, cinematic keynotes instead of live demos that went wrong. Computers got faster and phones thinner while people got lonelier. He still sometimes asks what Steve would do, quotes "the people who are crazy enough to think they can change the world, are the ones who do," and says there is still work worth putting a heart into. Nearby posts that day are about early Intel Mac OS X 10.4.3 and tinkering, in the same nostalgic register.

##### 要点

- The origin story is copying the iMac G4 onto a PC he could afford, which is how he learned UI.
- He misses live, fallible demos more than polished keynotes.
- The critique is incrementalism: thinner phones, more cameras, more scrolling, less heart.
- He would rather tools learn from people's lives than try to beat humans.
- The Jobs line he keeps is the "crazy enough to think they can change the world" quote.

#### 5. **A/B tests are traps**

**链接：** https://x.com/ryolu_/status/2106554858665033799
**时间 / 互动（如有）：** Sun, 04 Oct 2026 01:19:50 GMT; Likes=79 Replies=9 Reposts=5 Views=13234

##### 主帖在说什么

Replying to a question about whether A/B tests make the convergence problem worse, Lu says A/B tests are traps and distractions from the truth, and he does not believe in them. In the same conversation he added that making arbitrary small variants forks the path into oblivion with no sight of the full picture, and "a/b tests to oblivion." Elsewhere that weekend he wrote that copying before AI taught you why and how, and copying now means you do not think. The thread this sits under is the convergence essay.

##### 要点

- He rejects A/B testing outright, not just bad experiments.
- The failure mode is small variants that lose the full picture.
- He ties the practice to the convergence essay: measurement starts authoring the product.
- Pre-AI copying at least forced you to figure out why something worked.
