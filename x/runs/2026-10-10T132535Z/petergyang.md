#### 2. **Don’t name the Grok Bot email after yourself**

**链接：** https://x.com/petergyang/status/2108624122339287335
**时间 / 互动（如有）：** Fri, 09 Oct 2026 18:22:21 GMT; Likes=325 Replies=108 Views=51270

##### 主帖在说什么

Quoting Grok Bot’s announcement that the bot now has its own email and can sign up for services, contact businesses, or schedule time, Yang offers a setup tip. If the bot is a chief of staff, registering the mailbox as your own name is awkward, because the bot would “copy in Peter to find a time for us.” He suggests another name: whatever you want that chief of staff to be called. Later in the thread he jokes that copying a person named Trevin in order to schedule Trevin was a “gg noob mistake.” https://x.com/petergyang/status/2108646129156215214

##### 要点

- The product context is Grok Bot email, used as a chief of staff.
- His failure mode is the bot referring to you in the third person while using your name.
- The fix is a separate staff name, not a technical setting he documents.
- He does not discuss security of the bot sending mail.
