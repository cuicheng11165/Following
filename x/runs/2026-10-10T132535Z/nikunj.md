#### 1. **Three options for a seed founder near $1M**

**链接：** https://x.com/nikunj/status/2108410378233549106
**时间 / 互动（如有）：** Fri, 09 Oct 2026 04:13:01 GMT; Likes=547, Reposts=17, Replies=51, Bookmarks=498, Views=56370

##### 主帖在说什么

Nikunj says he had the same conversation at least twice a week, this time with a friend who has stellar customers, is getting close to $1M in revenue, and is running out of runway. The friend thinks the models have not caught up to the product, and feels the opportunity cost. Nikunj lists three options: stay a cockroach until default profitable, then raise big once demand shows up; jump to an orthogonal area that is already growing and where you have an unfair advantage; or sell or get acquihired into a more ambitious company and start again in one to two years. He says old Series A metrics do not matter in this consensus market, and that only a few categories have real VC pull. He says saying it out loud makes him uncomfortable.

##### 要点

- Concrete case: close to $1M revenue, not a rocket ship, dwindling runway, waiting on model capability.
- Option 1 is intense belief plus default profitability, then a large round.
- Option 2 is an orthogonal, faster category, and only if you have an unfair advantage.
- Option 3 is a sale or acquihire, then a new company in 1–2 years.
- He frames this as a description of the market, not a moral ranking.
