#### 5. **Embedded evaluators, like bank examiners**

**链接：** https://x.com/alexalbert__/status/2098814342443761909
**时间 / 互动（如有）：** Sat, 12 Sep 2026 16:41:47 GMT; Likes=189 Replies=57 Reposts=8 Views=27959

##### 主帖在说什么

Albert points readers at Dario Amodei's essay "We Must Pace the Frontier," in which Anthropic commits to giving third-party evaluators permanent, employee-level access so they can verify safety measures, report incidents, and assess alignment during training. Albert's argument is institutional, not novel policy: embedded evaluators sound unusual in tech but are normal elsewhere. Big banks have federal examiners with desks in the building, and every US nuclear plant has inspectors on site full time. He calls the same arrangement for frontier labs a practical first step.

##### 要点

- He asks people to read the essay in full.
- The analogy is bank examiners and on-site nuclear inspectors.
- He frames employee-level third-party access as ordinary oversight, not a strange tech idea.
- He calls it a practical first step, not the whole plan.
